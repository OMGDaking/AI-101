# Step 1: Quick Skim — Human

**Trigger:** I receive the Project 2 brief.

**Input:** The complete original brief, not a model summary.

1. Skim the original document once so I know what kind of document it is.
2. Note any immediate question or confusing passage. I do not have to choose the highlights yet.
3. Give the original brief (and any questions) to the model for Step 2.

**My check:** Have I looked at the original, not just a model summary?

**Good looks like:** I know the source the model will use and can recognize whether its notes follow that source.

**Output:** The original document plus any questions I noted. Questions are optional; the original document is required.
