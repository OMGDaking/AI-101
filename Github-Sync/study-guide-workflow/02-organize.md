# Step 2: Highlight, Note, and Organize — Model Assist

**Input:** The Project 2 brief and any questions from Step 1.

**Model's job:**

- Suggest highlights from the document and write short notes explaining each one. Label them as model suggestions, not my choices.
- Give each suggested highlight a type such as **requirement**, **deadline**, **term**, **example**, or **question** (use **key idea** or **process** where those fit better). Include the exact source section and paragraph or list item beside its note.
- Keep the highlights and notes in the same order as the source, with its section names. Do not reorder them by what the model thinks is important.
- Capture explicit requirements, dates, deliverables, and evaluation criteria without inventing missing details.
- Turn my questions, if any, into prompts for self-testing.
- Mark uncertainty or missing information instead of guessing.
- Leave space for my own explanations and priorities.

**Not the model's job:** Decide which requirements I can ignore, claim I understand a concept, or invent answers not supported by the brief.

**Output:** A draft of [[Github-Sync/study-guide-workflow/study-guide.md]] with suggested highlights and notes in source order, labeled **unreviewed** until I complete Step 3.

**Good looks like:** Every highlight has a useful type and a precise source location, notes are concise, source order is preserved, unknowns are marked, and my interpretation remains blank for me to write.

**How I check it:** I compare the outline against the original document and flag unsupported, missing, or misemphasized points for Step 3.
