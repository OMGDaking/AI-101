# Step 3: Human Check — Keep, Change, Cut

**This decision stays mine.** I compare every part of the draft study guide with the original Project 2 brief.

**Input:** The original brief, any questions I noted, and the model's unreviewed highlights and notes.

| Decision | What I look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep** | Accurate highlights and notes that help me study. |
| **Change** | A highlight that misses the point, a note that needs context, or wording I can explain better myself. |
| **Cut** | Unsupported or repetitive highlights and notes that do not help me learn. |

I record keep, change, and cut decisions in the study guide. I write the “My own understanding” section myself. If the draft conflicts with the brief, the brief wins.

For each note I keep, I ask: **Will I understand this a week from now?** If not, I rewrite it in my own words. I also check its type and source location against the original.

**Output:** A reviewed guide with my decisions and explanations, not just the model's outline.

**Good looks like:** Each kept fact is supported by the source; my own explanation and review decisions are visible.

**How I check it:** I can point to one keep, one change, and one cut with reasons in the guide's human-check record.
