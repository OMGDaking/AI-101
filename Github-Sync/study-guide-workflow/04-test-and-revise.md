# Step 4: Test and Revise — Human-Led

**Input:** My reviewed guide and the original Project 2 brief.

1. Hide the source and guide. Explain the Project 2 task, deliverables, and deadlines from memory.
2. Answer the guide's self-test questions in my own words without looking.
3. Check my answers against the original brief. Mark any missed, confused, or unsupported point.
4. Revise the guide to target those gaps; do not add material merely to make it longer.

**Evidence it helped:** I can explain the assignment accurately, identify what to do next, and answer the self-test questions without copying the guide. If I cannot, I repeat Steps 1–4 for the unclear sections.

**Good looks like:** The guide targets the gaps exposed by recall, rather than simply growing longer.

**How I check it:** I record what I recalled correctly, what I missed, and one revision that addresses a miss.

**Final output:** A study guide I have checked and can use, with unresolved questions clearly marked for the instructor.
