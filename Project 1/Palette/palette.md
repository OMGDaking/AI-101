# Three-color production palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| City field | Deep teal | `#276D6C` |
| Structure and silhouette | Dark wine | `#331517` |
| Signal and sky | Hot magenta | `#B5053B` |

This palette compresses the source images’ neon atmosphere into three flat screenprint colors. Teal carries depth, dark wine makes the figure and buildings feel heavy, and magenta supplies the electrical charge.
