Steinway & Sons Model B piano recordings from the University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples.
Performed by Evan Mazunik; recordings November 5 & 27, 2001; technician Michael Cash.
Created by Lawrence Fritts / University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.
Instrument details and source files: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISPiano.html
Usage statement: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
The collection states these recordings may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions.

Keyline's compact sample set uses 22 mezzo-forte recordings, converted from stereo 44.1 kHz AIFF to mono 22.05 kHz PCM WAV, with leading silence trimmed, gain adjusted and an eight-second maximum length with an end fade. Intermediate pitches are produced by playback-rate interpolation. A single recorded dynamic layer is used; velocity controls amplitude and filtering. This is not a complete multi-layer Steinway instrument library. No endorsement by Steinway & Sons is claimed.
