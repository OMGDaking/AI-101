Source: Berners, 1908, edited by A. Winterberger.

moonlight-first.mid is the unchanged first-movement MIDI from moonlight-mids.zip.
moonlight-score.pdf is the unchanged A4 score containing all three movements.
moonlight-first.json is a format conversion of first-movement MIDI note events
for Keyline playback, also licensed CC BY-SA 2.5. Pitches and timing are retained.
No original fingering inference or audio transcription is claimed.

Third movement (Presto agitato), same edition and license:
moonlight-third-original.mid is the unchanged moonlight3.mid from the same archive.
moonlight-third.mid and moonlight-third.json are Keyline format/tempo adaptations,
licensed CC BY-SA 2.5. All 4,863 source pitches, tick starts and note lengths are
retained; playback tempo changes from the MIDI's default 60 to 160 BPM.
Rhythmic proportions and source MIDI form/repeat handling are retained.
moonlight-page-08.png through moonlight-page-23.png render the third movement
from the unchanged full score PDF. The printed edition's fingerings are not
encoded by the hand animation, which uses suggested positions.
