# ADR-002 — Simple lessons and labeled hands
**Status** — AI-assisted retrospective, recorded November 19, 2026. Dakota review pending. Historical evidence comes from 2026-11-05 requests and November 10 context notes.
**Context** — Dakota said the lessons were messy and asked for keys, hands and playback with left/right labels.
**Decision** — Center practice on the keyboard and both hands. Put Lessons, Glove Locker, Import Music and Settings in a compact home menu.
**Consequence** — Reduces competing controls during learning. Detailed settings take an extra action.
