# ADR-003 — Revise hands after posture feedback
**Status** — AI-assisted retrospective, recorded November 19, 2026. Dakota review pending. Historical evidence comes from 74cea21 and e69796b, November 12; home feedback November 19.
**Context** — Repeated requests described disconnected, jagged or flat hands. November 19 home feedback again identified example hand posture.
**Decision** — Use separate left/right Blender hand rigs and preserve press/release animation. Treat anatomical and pedagogical accuracy as open until evaluated.
**Consequence** — Adds volume and independent hands. A visually smoother mesh does not validate technique.
