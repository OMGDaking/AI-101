# ADR-006 — Replace the human intro with shelves
**Status** — AI-assisted retrospective, recorded November 19, 2026. Dakota review pending. Historical evidence comes from 232504e, 56f1716 and 58e6d01, November 12.
**Context** — Dakota rejected the pianist model, then asked for an upright in an old building, falling objects and three loose shelves synchronized to a supplied chord recording.
**Decision** — Use a scripted Blender room, three splitting shelves, pencils, pens, tools and balls, with breaks on selected chord cues.
**Consequence** — Provides a coherent intro without a human model. The arrangement is reconstructed from the reference and the song identity remains undocumented.
