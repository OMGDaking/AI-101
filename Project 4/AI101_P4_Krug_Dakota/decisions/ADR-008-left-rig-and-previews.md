# ADR-008 — Left-hand curl and locker previews

**Status** — November 19 presentation revision requested by Dakota.
**Context** — Dakota reported poor left-hand motion and missing glove skins.
**Decision** — Bend the left finger chain across its joints and limit sideways knuckle movement. Use the existing illustrated hand and fabric pattern as a backup if a locker image fails.
**Consequence** — Both rigs displayed in Moonlight Sonata and all seven images decoded in the browser. The posture remains suggested fingering and physical microphone recognition needs testing.
