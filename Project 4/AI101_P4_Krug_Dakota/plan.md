# Keyline plan

Reconstructed with Codex on November 19, 2026 from Dakota's prior requests. These are documented intentions, not independently authored pre-build planning.

1. **Option** — Piano-learning app, with interactive keyboard, score and hand visualization. Instructor confirmation is needed because this is separate from the Project 3 study-guide agent.
2. **User** — Dakota Krug, learning piano at home and wanting a short demonstration followed by a chance to play the notes himself.
3. **Build sentence** — When Dakota chooses a lesson, Keyline demonstrates a short phrase with a keyboard and labeled hands, then waits for the expected notes before advancing in Try it.
4. **Boundary** — Imported music produces a draft to review. Hand positions are suggestions. Microphone input checks single notes and is not proof of chord accuracy. The app accepts local recordings and scores rather than retrieving songs from streaming services.
5. **Rejected idea** — Dakota rejected the animated suited pianist intro because the human model and hands looked poor. He chose an upright piano in an old room with falling objects instead. This is a documented design rejection, not evidence that three initial project ideas were written before building.
