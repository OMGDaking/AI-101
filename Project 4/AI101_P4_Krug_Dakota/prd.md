# Keyline PRD

AI-assisted retrospective, November 19, 2026. Scope approval under the Project 4 brief remains pending.

**Goal** — Help Dakota watch a piano phrase, try it, and repeat it at his own pace.

**User** — Dakota, practicing piano at home.

**Features**
1. The user can choose a progressive lesson, watch a short phrase, rewind it, and use Try it.
2. The user can see labeled left/right hands and highlighted keys, with speed controls and a score view.
3. The user can import local audio for an experimental piano draft and export MIDI, MusicXML or PDF.

**What happens** — Lessons opens a level menu. Selecting a lesson shows the keys and hands. Watch demonstrates a phrase and moves to Try it. A wrong on-screen note leaves the phrase waiting. Correct expected notes advance it. Local file import produces a reviewable draft. The optional intro uses a framed upright piano with shelves that break on chord cues.

**Look and feel** — Dark charcoal, warm ivory and muted piano-felt red. Compact bubble menu, simple lesson surface, smooth hands. The intro has separate landscape and portrait Full HD cuts at 30 fps.

**Out of scope** — Streaming downloads, account syncing, guaranteed note-perfect full-song transcription, professional fingering advice, and verified polyphonic microphone assessment.

**Done when**
1. A fresh opening can start the intro with audible playback enabled and then reach Lessons through Skip or the video's end.
2. At a 390 × 844 phone viewport, the intro selects the portrait cut and keeps the piano and three shelves in frame.
3. In lesson 1 Try it, D4 leaves progress unchanged and four separate C4 taps complete the phrase.
