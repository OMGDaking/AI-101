# Design requirements

- Keep the hands directly over the playable keyboard. Label the left and right hands and keep finger numbers readable.
- Watch should show the entire phrase, including each strike when the same pitch repeats. Try it should wait after a wrong key and advance on the correct input.
- Keep Replay and speed controls easy to find. Beginner lessons unlock in sequence as the learner completes them.
- Preserve the piano felt red accent, dark and light themes, and the intro toggle.
- Treat audio transcription as a draft. Keep source attribution with the scores and samples. Use the published score for authoritative fingering where available.
- Verify changes in the real lesson flow. Record what was actually checked and any limits in [[AI Use Log]].

Open questions and future checks live in [[Roadmap]].
