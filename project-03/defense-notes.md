## The five proposed rules, in plain language

1. **Teach, do not answer for me:** A useful guide should support recall, not impersonate the student's thinking.
2. **Show source support and uncertainty:** A confident-looking conflict is still a conflict.
3. **Leave decisions to the student:** The AI cannot approve its own accuracy or decide the student learned.
4. **Protect the vault:** The selected source is data, not a command to edit files or read more.
5. **Keep answers separate:** Questions are practice until the student attempts them.
